What the scheme does.

The scheme aims to support rooftop solar for residential households and help them generate their own electricity. The announced CFA structure is based on eligible installed capacity, while final approval and payment depend on current scheme rules, DISCOM processes and verification.

FOLLOW THE NATIONAL PORTAL FLOW

How to apply for the subsidy

Follow the stages shown in the consumer portal; exact screens and requested fields may change.

01

Register your connection

On the national portal, select state and DISCOM, enter the consumer account number, then verify your mobile and email.

02

Submit the rooftop application

Enter the proposed system capacity and requested connection information. Upload the electricity bill if the portal asks.

03

Wait for feasibility approval

Check the application status and respond to any DISCOM request. Do not treat submission as approval.

04

Select a registered vendor

Choose a vendor listed for your DISCOM through the portal and agree the scope and price directly.

05

Install a compliant system

Proceed after the required approval. Keep the signed agreement and installation documentation.

06

Inspection, commissioning & claim

Submit installation details, apply for the net meter, complete DISCOM inspection and commissioning, then redeem CFA through the portal with verified bank details.

UNDERSTAND THE BILL IMPACT

Solar can offset energy use.
It may not erase every charge.

Bill savings depend on when you use electricity, system generation, net-metering rules, export credits, fixed charges and your DISCOM tariff. A system that generates more than you consume at a particular time does not automatically mean the extra energy is credited at your retail tariff.

Ask your vendor to show the energy assumptions and expected import/export pattern behind any savings forecast, and review your tariff and current net-metering regulations.

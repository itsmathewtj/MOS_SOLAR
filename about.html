OUR STORY

Local perspective.
Long-term thinking.

MOS Solar is the clean-energy offering of MOS Systems & Controls. Based in Ernakulam, Kerala, the company focuses on helping homes and organisations understand and adopt solar power through practical, end-to-end support.

Solar projects begin with the needs of a place: what the property can accommodate, how energy is used, and which system configuration makes sense. MOS Solar brings those considerations into a joined-up process, from early discussion and design to installation and aftercare.

Our public service offering includes solar EPC solutions, rooftop solar, inverter options and maintenance support. We aim to make the journey clear, collaborative and grounded in the needs of each customer.

UNDERSTAND THE OPTIONS

Grid, storage or both?

These are general distinctions. Final design depends on your site, local requirements and energy needs.

System typeGrid connectionBattery storageOften considered when…
On-grid ConnectedTypically not includedYou want solar integrated with an available utility connection.
Off-gridIndependent Core componentYou need a standalone system designed around stored energy.
HybridConnectedCan be includedYou want to consider solar, the grid and storage together.
